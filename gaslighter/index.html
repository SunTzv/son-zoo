How to Play

Welcome to Gas is Lighter.

You are faced with a terribly drawn MS Paint image.

Your goal is to convince the Grumpy Professor (an AI) that the drawing is actually the Target Object.

The Confidence Meter: Watch the time of day! The game starts in the Morning (100% Professor Confidence). As you wear him down, the sky turns to Night (0% Confidence). Make it Night before you run out of attempts to win!

You have exactly 5 attempts.
